About

I'm Naraine — I build and manage websites for real clients, not just demo projects. Most days that means WordPress and Elementor, but I go wherever a project needs me to: from brand-accurate landing pages to full-stack tools that keep the work running smoothly behind the scenes.

Recent work has centered on two very different clients: 1GP, an Australian telehealth GP practice, and Mind Body Medras, a Chennai-based health education brand — each with its own colors, tone, and audience, and each built section by section from real briefs, not templates.

I also just finished a Python Fullstack Development certification from Greens Technology, and I've been building my own tools — like a React-based site monitor that keeps daily tabs on about 30 client websites so nothing breaks quietly.
